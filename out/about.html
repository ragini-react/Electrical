About PowerTech Electricals

Your Trusted Electrical Partner Since 2010

PowerTech Electricals is a leading electrical services company serving residential, commercial, and industrial clients across Madhya Pradesh. With over 13 years of experience, we have successfully completed thousands of projects ranging from simple home repairs to complex industrial installations.
